Each slider controls how much a semantic concept influences the 2D layout.

Slide up to spread apart items related to that concept. Slide down to 0 to collapse that distinction entirely.

Try it: set "Paper vs Blog" to 0 and watch publications and blog posts intermix. Or crank "Parsing" to 2 and see parsing papers fan out.

Layout: MDS computes positions that preserve pairwise distances (deterministic, smooth). Force-directed uses the distances as spring lengths and lets the simulation settle organically (more fluid, may find different structure).

Model switches between different embedding models, which may reveal different semantic structure.
